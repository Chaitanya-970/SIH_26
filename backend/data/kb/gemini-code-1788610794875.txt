================================================================================
MANGALORE REFINERY AND PETROCHEMICALS LIMITED (MRPL)
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: SOP-42 (REV 04)
TITLE: EMERGENCY MAINTENANCE AND APPROVAL CHAIN FOR ROTATING EQUIPMENT
================================================================================

1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This document defines the mandatory protocol for handling critical parameter breaches in rotating equipment (Pumps, Compressors, Turbines) across all operational refinery units.

2. VIBRATION AND TEMPERATURE THRESHOLDS
2.1 Vibration Severity (ISO 10816 Class III/IV Industrial Pumps):
    - Zone A/B (Normal): < 4.5 mm/s RMS.
    - Zone C (Alert): 4.5 mm/s to 7.1 mm/s RMS. Maintenance team must perform spectrum analysis within 24 hours.
    - Zone D (Critical/Trip): > 7.1 mm/s RMS. Immediate trip approval required from Shift In-Charge.

2.2 Bearing Temperature Thresholds:
    - Normal Range: 50.0°C to 75.0°C.
    - Alarm Threshold: 85.0°C.
    - Emergency Shutdown Threshold: 90.0°C. If drive end bearing exceeds 90.0°C for > 15 minutes, the equipment must be isolated immediately.

3. APPROVAL CHAIN & DELIVERABLES
For emergency maintenance requiring expenditure or spare parts allocation exceeding INR 50,000:
1. Field Inspector generates an Emergency Inspection Report.
2. Maintenance Engineer drafts a formal Approval Note containing:
   a) Equipment Tag & Location
   b) Root Cause Analysis (RCA) summary
   c) Estimated Downtime and Cost
   d) Signature Block for Chief Engineer approval.
3. Chief Engineer (Chaitanya) holds sole signatory authority for fast-track spares release under Emergency Procurement Scheme (EPS-2024).

4. LUBE OIL FLUSHING SPECIFICATIONS
Flush system using ISO VG 68 oil at 60°C for a minimum of 4 hours until particle counts meet ISO Cleanliness Code 16/14/11.
