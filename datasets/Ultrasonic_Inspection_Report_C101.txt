# Ultrasonic Thickness Inspection Report

**Asset ID:** Column C-101 (Crude Distillation Unit)
**Date of Inspection:** 2026-09-02
**Inspector:** V. Sharma, NDT Level III
**Equipment Type:** Fractionation Column
**Material:** Carbon Steel (SA-516 Gr. 70)

## 1. Scope of Inspection
Automated Ultrasonic Testing (AUT) was performed on the shell of Crude Column C-101, specifically focusing on the Heavy Gas Oil (HGO) draw-off section between Trays 14 and 18. The purpose of this inspection is to quantify localized wall thinning due to suspected naphthenic acid corrosion.

## 2. Findings and Measurements

*   **Original Nominal Thickness (T_nom):** 14.0 mm
*   **Minimum Required Thickness (T_min):** 7.2 mm (per ASME Sec VIII Div 1)
*   **Previous Reading (2024):** 12.1 mm
*   **Current Minimum Reading (2026):** 8.4 mm

### 2.1 Zone Breakdown (Trays 14-18)
| Location (Elevation) | T_nom | Current Reading | Corrosion Rate (mm/yr) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Tray 14 (South) | 14.0 | 9.8 mm | 0.25 |
| Tray 15 (East) | 14.0 | 9.1 mm | 0.31 |
| Tray 16 (North) - Weld Seam | 14.0 | 8.4 mm | 0.42 |
| Tray 17 (West) | 14.0 | 8.9 mm | 0.35 |
| Tray 18 (Draw Nozzle) | 14.0 | 8.6 mm | 0.39 |

## 3. Analysis
The highest observed corrosion rate is 0.42 mm/year near the Tray 16 weld seam. At the current remaining thickness of 8.4 mm, the time to reach the retirement limit (7.2 mm) is approximately 2.8 years (34 months).

## 4. Recommendations
Given the accelerated localized thinning exceeding 0.3 mm/year, it is highly recommended to plan for a metallurgical upgrade. 
**Action Item:** Schedule internal weld overlay cladding (Type 317L Stainless Steel) for the Tray 14-18 section during the next planned turnaround window to mitigate further high-temperature sulfidation and naphthenic acid attack.
