# Standard Operating Procedure: SOP-402
**Title:** Asset Integrity & Corrosion Management
**Department:** Reliability & Mechanical Engineering
**Revision:** 4.1

## 1. Purpose
This SOP dictates the evaluation criteria for pressure vessels and piping subject to continuous high-temperature or highly corrosive environments within the refinery limits.

## 2. Thickness Limits & Retirement (Section VIII)
All carbon steel pressure vessels must maintain a Minimum Required Thickness (T_min) calculated based on maximum allowable working pressure (MAWP) according to ASME Section VIII Div 1.
*   If current thickness falls within **1.5 mm** of T_min, the asset is flagged as **CRITICAL**.
*   If current thickness falls below T_min, the asset must be taken **OUT OF SERVICE** immediately.

## 3. Corrosion Velocity Guidelines
The annualized corrosion rate is determined by comparing current AUT readings with the historical baseline.
*   **Low:** < 0.15 mm/year
*   **Moderate:** 0.15 - 0.25 mm/year
*   **High (Action Required):** > 0.25 mm/year

## 4. Turnaround Approvals for High Corrosion
If a vessel exhibits a High Corrosion Velocity (>0.25 mm/year), the Reliability Engineer must draft an **Executive Turnaround Approval Note**.
This note must contain:
1.  Asset ID and Location.
2.  Current minimum thickness reading.
3.  Calculated months remaining until T_min breach.
4.  A recommended metallurgical mitigation (e.g., alloy upgrading or internal cladding).

## 5. Vibration & Rotating Equipment Safety (Addendum B)
For rotating equipment (e.g., Boiler Feed Water Pumps, Booster Pumps):
*   **Vibration Alarm (RMS):** 4.5 mm/s
*   **Vibration Trip/Shutdown:** 7.1 mm/s (per ISO 10816-3)
*   **Bearing Temperature Trip:** 95°C

Repeated trips due to seal failures require a root cause analysis and a mandatory upgrade of the seal flush plan (e.g., Plan 31 to Plan 53B) before restarting.
